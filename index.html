rclone Personal Drive Access

This is a private, single-user OAuth client used by its owner, Sergey Forin, to connect the open-source tool rclone to his own Google Drive account on his personal Linux computer.

What the app does

The app lets rclone list, upload, download and sync files in the owner's Google Drive, and mount the drive as a local folder for transferring files between the owner's own devices.

Why it requests Google Drive access

Full Drive access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) is required so that files added to the drive from any of the owner's devices are visible and can be copied. The app is not offered to, or intended for, any other users.
